# 0012. Meters and readings

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Feature: issue-52
- Source: [`docs/00-analisis-inicial.md`](../00-analisis-inicial.md), section 7.2 ("Lecturas de medidor") and the priority "Horómetros"; migration [`0012_metering.sql`](../../db/migrations/0012_metering.sql); decisions D1 to D12 in [`odd/tasks/issue-52-meter-readings.md`](../../odd/tasks/issue-52-meter-readings.md); builds on [ADR-0005](0005-cross-module-authorization.md), [ADR-0007](0007-fleet-catalogs.md), [ADR-0010](0010-equipment-assignments.md) and [ADR-0011](0011-equipment-image.md)

## Context

The asset type already says whether an equipment measures in hours, kilometers or nothing, but there was nowhere to record what the equipment reads. Control lived in spreadsheets with negative readings, text in numeric cells and irregular periods. Hours of use are the base of preventive maintenance, of repair orders and of checking what a supplier bills.

## Decision

- **A module of its own.** `src/metering` (the `metering` module of the analysis document) holds meters and readings. It never imports `fleet` or `organization`. It reaches the equipment through the shared-kernel port `EquipmentDirectory`, which `fleet` implements: the meter kind of the asset type, whether it is archived and the position of its open assignment, with an optional `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` lock of the equipment row (the same lock as every other writer of one equipment, ADR-0010).
- **Model.** `meters` (one OPEN meter per equipment, a partial unique index) and `meter_readings` (value in tenths, day, kind `reading`, `correction`, `replacement_close` or `replacement_open`, the reading a correction refers to, a reason, who loaded it). Days are `date`, never a JavaScript `Date`. A tie-breaking identity column `seq` orders readings of the same day and instant.
- **Immutable by the database.** `parkline_app` can only `SELECT` and `INSERT` readings: update, delete and truncate are revoked. A meter can be read, created and closed: the application role may update ONLY its column `closed_on`, so the meter's kind, opening day and initial value cannot be rewritten even by a bug (the default table-level `UPDATE` privilege is revoked first).
- **Rules.** A reading is a non-negative value with one decimal (at most 9,999,999.9), not in the future, and it must fit between its neighbors in the order of `(day, creation)`. A LOWER value is never accepted as a reading: it is a meter replacement, an explicit event with a mandatory reason that closes the old meter with its final value and opens a new one with an initial value, in one transaction. A correction inserts a new reading that refers to the original (a chain always refers to the original) and never edits it; the effective value of a reading is its latest correction; a correction must also fit between the neighbors and needs a reason. The hours of a period are the difference of effective values per meter segment, summed; a segment with fewer than two usable readings contributes nothing and the result is reported as incomplete instead of inventing a number.
- **Read-only roles (decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-24).** `reading.write` (load) and `reading.correct` (correct and replace) belong only to `admin` and `fleet_analyst`. `supervisor`, `manager` and `viewer` read, through `catalog.read` with the visibility by open assignment (ADR-0010 D6): a missing equipment and one the actor cannot see answer the same `not_found`. This replaces the analysis document's "supervisor (OR y lecturas de su alcance)" for readings; the document was updated.
- **Audit.** `metering.reading.create` is not sensitive; `metering.reading.correct` and `metering.meter.replace` are sensitive (in `SENSITIVE_AUDIT_ACTIONS` and in the trigger function, replaced by the same migration) and need a reason.
- **The first reading at registration.** The registration dialog can carry an initial reading. `fleet` must not import `metering`, so the dependency is inverted with the shared-kernel port `InitialReadingRecorder`: `fleet`'s `CreateEquipment` calls the port, `metering` implements it, and the container builds the implementation over the SAME SQL handle as `fleet`'s transaction. The equipment, its image, its assignment, its meter and its first reading are one transaction. An asset type without meter refuses it with `no_meter`, and any refusal creates nothing.
- **Screens.** A Lecturas tab in `/fleet/equipment/<id>?tab=readings` (open meter, hours of the last 30 days, history newest first with a "Corregida" marker) and the last reading in the information card; dialogs to load, correct and replace.

## Consequences

- A history is read as its newest 5000 readings. At one reading a day that is about 13 years of one equipment. Past that, a very old backdated reading would be checked against an incomplete history and a reading older than the window cannot be corrected. The bound keeps every operation cheap; raising it is a one-line change.
- Concurrency is proven against real Postgres: two readings, a reading and a correction, a replacement and a reading, and a reading and the archive of the equipment end consistent; without the equipment lock the five race tests fail.
- The audit entry of the first reading is written before the entries of the equipment and its assignment of the same registration, because the port runs before `fleet` writes them. Inside the one transaction the three commit together.
- The column "who loaded it" shows names only to actors that may list users (company-wide `user.read`); everyone else sees their own rows as "Tú" and a dash for the rest. A port of user names would remove that limit.
- The migration adds two tables and replaces the trigger function. It is backward compatible, but the new code reads the new tables, so it must be applied to the hosted database from the maintainer machine (`pnpm db:migrate` with the owner URL) BEFORE the pull request is merged, and only with the maintainer's explicit authorization. Cost: none.
- Out of scope, tracked elsewhere: the preventive traffic light (Vigente, Próximo a vencer, Vencido), importing the providers' declarations, utilization against contracted hours, and rates.
