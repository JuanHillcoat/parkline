## Objective

An equipment whose asset type measures in hours or km gets a meter; people load immutable readings, correct them and replace the meter, always with an audit trail; the hours of a period are computed from the readings.

## Design decisions

- D1 New module `src/metering/{domain,application/{use-cases,ports},infrastructure,__tests__}` (the `metering` module of the analysis document), added to `eslint.architecture.mjs` with its import rules and explained in `src/README.md`. It never imports `fleet` or `organization`. It reaches the equipment through a new shared-kernel port `EquipmentDirectory` (like `OperationDirectory` and `AssignmentDirectory`) that `fleet` implements: given a tenant and an equipment id it returns `{ id, meterKind, archived, position }` where `position` is the scope position of its open assignment (or none), and takes the equipment row lock `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` when asked (same lock as the other writers of one equipment). `metering` authorizes through `AccessPolicy` at that position, before any other lookup.
- D2 Model: `meters(id, tenant_id, equipment_id, kind hours|km, opened_on, closed_on null, initial_value)` (one OPEN meter per equipment, partial unique index) and `meter_readings(id, tenant_id, meter_id, read_on day, value numeric(10,1), kind reading|correction|replacement_close|replacement_open, corrects_id null, reason null, created_by, created_at)`. Readings are immutable: `parkline_app` gets select and insert only, no update, no delete (explicit revoke, ADR-0004). Days are `YYYY-MM-DD` text or date like assignments (no JavaScript `Date`). Business tables carry `tenant_id`; composite foreign keys keep rows in their tenant.
- D3 Rules: the meter kind comes from the asset type (`hours` or `km`; `none` has no meter: `no_meter`). The first reading of an equipment opens its meter. A reading is `0 <= value`, one decimal, at most 9,999,999.9, `read_on` not in the future (clock port). Readings are ordered by `(read_on, created_at)`; a new reading must be `>=` the effective value before it in that order and `<=` the effective value after it (a backdated reading must fit between its neighbors): otherwise `reading_out_of_order`. A lower value is NOT accepted as a reading: it must be a meter replacement (the typed error says so). Same-day readings are allowed.
- D4 Correction: `CorrectReading(readingId, newValue, reason)` inserts a `correction` reading with `corrects_id`; the effective value of a reading is its latest correction. The corrected value must fit between its neighbors (same rule). Correcting a correction corrects the original chain. Mandatory reason.
- D5 Replacement: `ReplaceMeter(equipmentId, day, finalValue, initialValue, reason)` inserts `replacement_close` (final value of the old meter on that day, must be `>=` its last effective value) and closes the meter, opens a new meter and inserts `replacement_open` (initial value, any `>= 0`), in one transaction. Mandatory reason.
- D6 Hours of a period: `hoursBetween(from, to)` per meter segment = effective value at `to` minus effective value at `from` (the last reading on or before each day, within the segment), summed over segments; a segment with fewer than two usable readings contributes nothing and the result is reported as `incomplete`. A pure domain function with its own tests.
- D7 Permissions (changed by the maintainer on 2026-10-24: "el supervisor y gerente de operacion solo debe tener permisos de lectura. No puedo modificar valores en los equipos"): new permissions `reading.write` (load) and `reading.correct` (correct and replace), BOTH only for `admin` and `fleet_analyst`. `supervisor` and `manager` are read-only: they read through `catalog.read` with the visibility by open assignment (ADR-0010 D6) through `EquipmentDirectory`; a missing or invisible equipment answers the same `not_found` (issue 16 pattern). This overrides the analysis document's "supervisor (OR y lecturas de su alcance)" for readings. An archived equipment accepts no new readings (`equipment_archived`).
- D8 Audit: `metering.reading.create` (not sensitive), `metering.reading.correct` and `metering.meter.replace` (sensitive: in `SENSITIVE_AUDIT_ACTIONS` and in the trigger function, in the same migration). Entries carry the equipment as subject, the values and the reason.
- D9 Screens: a Lecturas tab in `/fleet/equipment/<id>` with the history (newest first: day, value, kind pill, who, reason, a "corregida" marker for superseded readings), the buttons Cargar lectura, Corregir (per row) and Reemplazar medidor according to permission, the hours of the last 30 days, and the last reading in the information card. An equipment whose type has no meter shows "Este tipo de activo no tiene medidor". Spanish copy in `app/_lib/copy.ts`.
- D10 Concurrency: every writer takes the equipment row lock through `EquipmentDirectory` before reading the readings it decides on; two concurrent readings on one equipment cannot both pass the order check. Authorization before any lock.
- D11 First reading at registration (changed on 2026-10-24: "con respecto a la carga de horas en el alta de equipo busquemosle una solucion porque debe estar"): a shared-kernel port `InitialReadingRecorder` (in-transaction, like `OperationDirectory` and `AssignmentDirectory`) implemented by `metering` and injected into `fleet`'s `CreateEquipment`, so the equipment, its optional assignment (issue 50), its optional image and its first reading are ONE transaction and `fleet` never imports `metering`. The dialog gets an optional "Lectura inicial" (value and day, shown when the chosen asset type has a meter); the asset type `none` refuses it with `no_meter`; it needs the same permission as the registration (`admin`, `fleet_analyst`).
- D12 Out of this delivery: the preventive traffic light, importing provider declarations, utilization against contracted hours, rates.
