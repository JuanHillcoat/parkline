## Objective

Registering an equipment asks for its battery type, its meter reading and where it enters (an operation, or a site as a spare) in one compact screen; a spare belongs to a site, not to an operation.

## Design decisions

- D1 Battery: new column `equipments.battery_kind` (`lithium`, `lead_acid`, `combustion`, nullable for old rows) and a domain value. Required when creating and editing (like brand and model); old rows with null stay readable and archivable. `battery_quantity` (0 to 4, optional) is allowed ONLY for `lead_acid`; for the other kinds it must be empty (typed error if given, the screen never sends it). Audit snapshots, list items, detail, edit dialog and copy carry the kind.
- D2 A spare belongs to a SITE: in `equipment_assignments`, `operation_id` and `business_unit_id` become nullable. A `spare` assignment now targets a site (its operation and division are null); an `active` assignment still requires an operation (and its division and site). Checks: `kind = 'active'` implies operation and division not null; operation and division are both null or both not null. Existing rows (a spare inside an operation, from before) stay valid and readable, but no use case creates one any more. The use cases take a TARGET: an operation (kind active) or a site (kind spare); the kind is derived from the target.
- D3 Operations on the target: assign, move, correct and create-with-assignment accept either target; moving goes from an operation to another operation or to a site as a spare and back; the correction changes the target the same way. Replacement "leave as a spare" puts the outgoing equipment as a spare at the SITE of the operation it leaves (operation null); "retire" is unchanged. The exclusion constraint (no overlap per equipment) is unchanged.
- D4 Position and visibility: the position of a site-level spare is `{ tenantId, siteId }` (no division, no operation). Authorization and the D6 visibility of ADR-0010 therefore reach it only for actors whose grants cover the whole company (a scope on a division or an operation never matches it); the readings of metering use the same position. This is a stated limitation: a division or operation supervisor or manager does not see the spares of their site yet; to revisit with the maintainer.
- D5 Blocks: an archived site cannot receive a spare; a site with open spare assignments cannot be archived (`has_open_assignments`, through the existing shared-kernel `AssignmentDirectory`, which learns sites); an operation's block is unchanged.
- D6 Registration form (S4): fields in the order of the specs, with battery kind first among the battery fields and the quantity shown only for lead acid; "Horómetro" (labeled "Kilometraje" for a km type) shown only when the chosen type has a meter; "Operación" select with every active operation plus "Muleto"; choosing "Muleto" shows a "Site" select (every active site); "Desde el día" (today by default) is the day of the assignment AND of the meter reading; "Observaciones" is the last field and is also the reason of the assignment (mandatory when the day is in the past, with a message that names Observaciones). The old "Asignación (opcional)" group, the kind select, the separate reading day and the separate reason field disappear. The operation select may stay empty (the equipment is registered unassigned).
- D7 Compact layout: a wide dialog with a multi-column grid so every field fits a desktop viewport of 1280 x 800 without scrolling the dialog; one column on mobile (400 x 608, scroll allowed). No hint under "Interno".
- D8 Wording: every Spanish label, menu, tab, message and test that says "Tipo de activo" says "Tipo de equipo". Identifiers in code keep `assetType`.
- D9 The assign and move dialogs of the detail page use the same target select (operations plus Muleto and its Site); the board and the detail show a spare as "Muleto" with its site and an empty operation.
- D10 No change to permissions; metering position rules follow D4.
